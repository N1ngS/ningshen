Jianlibao — “Yi” Jian Jiang Hu (倚”健”江湖) 🧃

Client

Jianlibao Sports Drink

My Role

Campaign Planner | Market Research | Content Design | Media Planer | Budget Management | Poster and Key Vision Design

Background

Consumer research revealed that although Jianlibao remained a well-known heritage brand, its appeal among younger audiences had gradually declined due to an outdated brand image. The campaign aimed to revitalise the brand by connecting traditional Chinese cultural identity with contemporary youth interests.

Insight

Gen Z consumers are drawn to brands that combine cultural identity with interactive experiences, making traditional brands more relevant when reimagined through contemporary storytelling.

Objective

Reposition Jianlibao, a heritage Chinese beverage brand, by integrating traditional Chinese wuxia culture with contemporary youth lifestyles to reconnect with Generation Z and strengthen long-term brand relevance.

Strategy

Developed an integrated online-to-offline campaign centred on the “Yijian Jianghu” concept, combining Guochao-inspired martial arts storytelling with interactive social media content, experiential events, and cultural partnerships. By connecting traditional Chinese culture with contemporary youth lifestyles, the campaign refreshed Jianlibao’s brand image, strengthened emotional engagement with Gen Z consumers, and increased product awareness, participation, and purchase intention.

Tactics

1.Interactive Digital Experience through personalised quizzes and branded content.
2.Integrated Social Media Campaign to increase engagement and user participation.
3.Experiential Marketing Events inspired by traditional Chinese martial arts and contemporary youth culture.

Market & Consumer Analysis

Strategic research examining Jianlibao’s market landscape, brand positioning, competitive environment and consumer behaviour. The findings provide actionable insights to support campaign development and strengthen the brand’s connection with Generation Z consumers.

Aihuashi — Dream Collectors (梦想收藏家) 🧳

Client

Aihuashi Luggage

My Role

Campaign Planner | Strategy Development | Consumer Research

Background

Market and consumer research showed that while Aihuashi had established strong product quality and brand recognition, it lacked emotional differentiation among younger consumers. The campaign sought to transform functional product value into an emotional brand experience centred around dreams, memories and travel.

Insight

Gen Z consumers value meaningful experiences over functional products alone. They are more likely to connect with brands that inspire self-expression, preserve memories, and encourage them to pursue their dreams through travel.

Objective

Reposition Aihuashi as an emotionally engaging lifestyle brand by strengthening its connection with Generation Z through a storytelling-driven integrated marketing campaign.

Strategy

Inspired by the post-pandemic recovery of travel and the growing demand for meaningful experiences, the campaign positioned every journey as an opportunity to collect dreams, memories, and life experiences through integrated online and offline storytelling.

Tactics

1.Interactive Digital Campaigns featuring H5 experiences, quizzes, and user-generated storytelling to encourage participation.
2.Social Media & Brand Collaborations with creators and lifestyle partners to amplify the campaign message and drive engagement.
3.Experiential Brand Activations including exhibitions, travel-inspired events, and public initiatives that connected emotional storytelling with real-world experiences.

The popularization plan of Vita Oolong Tea (维它乌龙茶推广计划) 🍵

Client

Weita Oolong Tea

My Role

Team Leader | Campaign Planning | Project Coordination | Consumer Research

Background

Research showed that demand for healthier, low-sugar beverages was growing, while university students were increasingly influenced by social media, product sampling, and interactive brand experiences. The campaign was designed to increase awareness of Vitasoy Oolong Tea and strengthen its competitive position in the campus beverage market.

Insight

University students are increasingly choosing healthier drinks, but they are more likely to try new products through interactive experiences, peer recommendations, and social media engagement than traditional advertising.

Objective

Increase awareness and trial of Vitasoy Oolong Tea among university students by promoting its zero-sugar, healthy lifestyle positioning and encouraging product engagement on campus.

Strategy

Develop an integrated campus campaign combining experiential marketing, product sampling, and social media engagement to encourage product trial, strengthen brand awareness, and generate authentic consumer interactions.

Tactics

1.Campus Product Sampling to encourage first-time product trials and collect consumer feedback.
2.Interactive On-campus Activities featuring games, lucky draws, and branded experiences to increase student participation.
3.Social Media Promotion encouraging students to share their experiences and expand campaign reach through user-generated content.

Yunnan Baiyao — “Four Seasons Oral Garden”(口腔花园计划) 🦷

Client

YunnanBaiyao Toothpaste

My Role

Campaign Planning | Consumer Research | Project Coordination

Background

Although Yunnan Baiyao is a trusted oral care brand with strong product efficacy, competition in the toothpaste market continues to intensify. Younger consumers increasingly expect brands to provide engaging experiences and emotional value beyond functional benefits, creating an opportunity to strengthen long-term brand engagement.

Insight

Consumers care about effective oral care, but they are more likely to build lasting relationships with brands that transform everyday routines into enjoyable, interactive experiences.

Objective

Strengthen Yunnan Baiyao’s emotional connection with young consumers by transforming daily oral care into an engaging year-round brand experience through an integrated digital and experiential marketing campaign.

Strategy

Develop the “Four Seasons Oral Garden” campaign, positioning oral care as nurturing a personal garden throughout the year. By integrating gamified digital experiences, seasonal storytelling, and offline brand activations, the campaign encouraged continuous consumer participation while reinforcing Yunnan Baiyao’s professional oral care positioning.

Tactics

1.Gamified Digital Experience through a mini-program where users grow and personalise their virtual oral garden with daily interactions and rewards.
2.Seasonal Integrated Marketing Campaigns featuring themed social content, KOL collaborations, public initiatives, and user-generated content across the four seasons.
3.Experiential Brand Activations including pop-up exhibitions, community oral health events, and interactive offline experiences to deepen consumer engagement.

Dakang Laundry Detergent Integrated Marketing Campaign(一“净”到底) 👔

Client

Dakang Laundry Powder

My Role

Campaign Planner | Consumer Research | Strategy Development | Creative Concept

Background

As one of China’s most recognised household cleaning brands, Diaopai has built strong awareness through its trusted cleaning performance and value-for-money positioning. However, its traditional marketing approach has struggled to resonate with younger consumers, despite growing demand for premium, health-focused laundry products with antibacterial and anti-mite benefits.

Insight

Young consumers increasingly value healthier lifestyles and premium home care experiences, but they are more likely to engage with brands that combine functional product benefits with creative storytelling and interactive digital experiences.

Objective

Reposition Diaopai as a modern household care brand by increasing awareness of its antibacterial and anti-mite technology while strengthening emotional engagement with younger consumers through an integrated online-to-offline campaign.

Strategy

Develop the “Yi Jing Dao Di” (一jing到底) campaign by using a memorable wordplay concept to communicate Diaopai’s core antibacterial technology. Through influencer marketing, immersive brand storytelling, factory transparency, and experiential offline activations, the campaign reinforced product credibility while refreshing Diaopai’s image among young families and Generation Z consumers.

Tactics

1.Social Media & Influencer Campaign featuring lifestyle and review creators across Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Douyin, and Kuaishou to spark discussions around antibacterial laundry care.
2.Factory Transparency Content showcasing Diaopai’s production process and SRP antibacterial technology through behind-the-scenes video storytelling to build consumer trust.
3.Offline Experiential Activations including educational roadshows, family-friendly games, and shopping mall events promoting healthy home environments and anti-mite awareness.
