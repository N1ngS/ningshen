Jianlibao — “Yi Jian Jiang Hu” (倚”健”江湖)

Client

Jianlibao Sports Drink

Objective

Reposition Jianlibao, a heritage Chinese beverage brand, by integrating traditional Chinese wuxia culture with contemporary youth lifestyles to reconnect with Generation Z and strengthen long-term brand relevance.

Background

Consumer research revealed that although Jianlibao remained a well-known heritage brand, its appeal among younger audiences had gradually declined due to an outdated brand image. The campaign aimed to revitalise the brand by connecting traditional Chinese cultural identity with contemporary youth interests.

Aihuashi — Dream Collectors (梦想收藏家)

Client

Aihuashi Luggage

Objective

Reposition Aihuashi as an emotionally engaging lifestyle brand by strengthening its connection with Generation Z through a storytelling-driven integrated marketing campaign.

Background

Market and consumer research showed that while Aihuashi had established strong product quality and brand recognition, it lacked emotional differentiation among younger consumers. The campaign sought to transform functional product value into an emotional brand experience centred around dreams, memories and travel.

Strategy

Inspired by the post-pandemic recovery of travel and the growing demand for meaningful experiences, the campaign positioned every journey as an opportunity to collect dreams, memories and life experiences through integrated online and offline activations and emotional storytelling.

Outcome

Developed a comprehensive integrated marketing campaign combining consumer insights, emotional storytelling and multi-channel activation to strengthen Aihuashi’s emotional brand positioning.

Yunnan Baiyao — “Four Seasons Oral Garden”(口腔花园计划)

Client

YunnanBaiyao Toothpaste

Objective

Develop an integrated marketing campaign to reposition Yunnan Baiyao among younger consumers through gamification, seasonal storytelling and interactive brand experiences.

Background

The campaign transformed oral healthcare into an engaging digital experience that encouraged long-term consumer participation while modernising the brand image.

Strategy

Inspired by the idea that oral health requires continuous care, the campaign combined a WeChat Mini Program, seasonal marketing activities and gamified interactions to build emotional connections with consumers.

Outcome

Developed an integrated campaign combining consumer insights, digital engagement, experiential marketing and brand storytelling to strengthen Yunnan Baiyao’s relevance among younger audiences.
