™️ Brand Strategy & Consumer Culture Analysis

Overview

Critically analysed whether brands can truly be managed in today’s digital environment by examining the relationship between brand strategy, stakeholder engagement, and consumer co-creation. Drawing on cultural branding theories and case studies of Apple, Gucci, and Arc’teryx, the essay argues that while organisations can strategically guide brand direction, brand meaning is ultimately co-created through public participation, media discourse, and digital culture.

Key Topics

Brand as Asset vs. Brand as Cultural System | Stakeholder Co-creation | Cultural Branding | Consumer Participation | Digital Brand Management | Apple, Gucci & Arc’teryx Case Studies

🌍 Experiential Marketing: Klimahaus Bremerhaven

Overview

This team project explored how Klimahaus Bremerhaven, an immersive climate experience museum in Germany, applies experiential marketing and creative communication to engage visitors with climate change. Rather than relying on traditional educational displays, the museum creates an interactive journey through multiple climate zones, combining storytelling, sensory experiences and environmental simulation to strengthen audience engagement and emotional connection.
