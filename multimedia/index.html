The second project investigates graphic design principles through book covers, artworks, architecture and original photography. By applying concepts such as hierarchy, contrast, repetition, balance and spatial organisation, the project demonstrates how design decisions influence perception and communication.

Together, these projects reflect my ability to critically analyse visual media while developing a strong foundation in graphic design theory, composition and multimedia communication.
