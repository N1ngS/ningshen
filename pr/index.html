Haidilao PR Crisis Analysis 🍲

This academic paper analyses Haidilao’s 2025 public relations crisis from a stakeholder perspective. It examines how communication priorities, stakeholder management and two-way communication influenced the company’s crisis response and reputation.

Drawing on Stakeholder Theory, the Linkage Model and the Two-Way Symmetric Model, the paper evaluates Haidilao’s communication strategy and proposes more effective stakeholder-centred crisis communication approaches.

DR Jewellery 💎

Crisis Communication Analysis

DR (Darry Ring) faced a major public relations crisis in 2021 after concerns emerged over customer privacy and purchase records linked to its "One Person for Life" branding. The company quickly denied rumors, suspended its verification system, and promised security improvements. However, its response was criticized for focusing on defending the brand rather than addressing consumers' privacy concerns, offering limited transparency and insufficient efforts to rebuild public trust.

Analysed the communication response to the DR Jewellery brand crisis, evaluating stakeholder reactions, media framing, crisis response and reputation management.
