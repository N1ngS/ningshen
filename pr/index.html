Stakeholder Engagement Campaign: Open the Door to Care: See the people, process, and care behind every decision 🐶

Client

RSPCA Australia : Australia’s national LGBTQIA+ youth charity dedicated to creating an Australia free from discrimination. Minus18 supports LGBTQIA+ young people through school inclusion programs, youth events, educational resources, and advocacy initiatives.

My Role

PR Strategist | Campaign Planner | Stakeholder Researcher | Presentation Designer

Background

Despite Minus18’s ongoing advocacy, rural LGBTQIA+ young people continue to experience discrimination and limited access to support services. Research shows that many students regularly experience verbal abuse, few feel safe to come out, and most regional communities still lack LGBTQIA+ support. However, mainstream media attention has declined, reducing public awareness of these ongoing issues.

Insight

Urban Australians generally support LGBTQIA+ equality, yet many remain unaware of the challenges faced by young people in regional communities. By combining authentic personal stories with credible research and trusted media partnerships, the campaign can transform rural inclusion from a niche issue into a shared national conversation.

Objective

Increase public awareness and media attention surrounding rural LGBTQIA+ youth by engaging mainstream media, influential advocates, and urban audiences. The campaign aims to strengthen public empathy, encourage donations, and build long-term media relationships that support Minus18’s advocacy goals.

Strategy

Develop a targeted media relations campaign that leverages mainstream journalists, influential LGBTQIA+ advocates, and trusted public voices. By combining compelling human-interest storytelling with research-based evidence, the campaign positions rural LGBTQIA+ inclusion as a national social issue deserving sustained media attention and public support.

Tactics

1.Designed media outreach materials including press releases, opinion pieces, journalist briefings, and customised media kits featuring first-person stories, research findings, and visual content tailored to target journalists.
2.Built strategic relationships with key media outlets, community leaders, and LGBTQIA+ advocates through exclusive briefings, policy information packs, and collaborative media opportunities to strengthen campaign credibility.
3.Developed public engagement initiatives including awareness campaigns, online donation integration, journalist roundtables, and virtual impact tours to increase campaign visibility, encourage donations, and sustain long-term media coverage.

JALA Group Employer Branding PR Campaign 🎓

Client

JALA Group (伽蓝集团) : One of China’s leading cosmetics groups, integrating research, manufacturing, sales, and services. JALA owns multiple beauty and skincare brands and aims to strengthen its employer brand to attract and retain young talent while reinforcing its corporate values and long-term career opportunities.

My Role

Public Relations Planner (Team Leader)

Background

Although JALA is well established within China’s beauty industry, its corporate employer brand remains relatively unknown among young job seekers. Research found that many graduates are unfamiliar with the company, while existing recruitment communication lacks differentiation and has limited visibility across platforms frequently used by Generation Z. In addition, the connection between JALA’s corporate brand and its well-known product brands is not sufficiently recognised.

Insight

Today’s graduates value more than salary when choosing employers. They seek career development, a sense of belonging, meaningful workplace culture, and opportunities to grow with like-minded colleagues. Building an employer brand that highlights career support, community, and personal development can create stronger emotional connections with young professionals.

Objective

Increase awareness of JALA’s employer brand among university students and recent graduates by positioning the company as an attractive workplace that offers career growth, supportive culture, and long-term development opportunities. The campaign also aims to improve employer brand recognition and strengthen recruitment effectiveness.

Strategy

Position JALA as “the best career companion for young job seekers” by integrating employer branding with youth-oriented storytelling and interactive recruitment experiences. The campaign combines digital communication, campus engagement, and employee value proposition (EVP) messaging to enhance emotional connection and encourage graduates to view JALA as a preferred employer.

Tactics

1.Developed an integrated employer branding campaign centred on the theme “The Best JALA Partner”, using social media content, campus communication, and storytelling to increase awareness among university students and young professionals.
2.Planned interactive recruitment activities including campus events, career-sharing sessions, online engagement, and employer branding content across platforms such as Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and Bilibili to improve visibility and participation.
3.Designed communication materials highlighting JALA’s career development programs, employee benefits, workplace culture, and clear promotion pathways to strengthen employer credibility and encourage graduate applications.

Crisis Communication Analysis of DR Jewellery 💎

Client

DR (Darry Ring) : A premium jewellery brand in China best known for its signature proposition, “One person, one lifetime, one DR ring.” This project analysed the brand’s public relations crisis following controversy surrounding its pricing, purchase records, and crisis communication strategy, evaluating how the company’s response affected brand trust and public perception.

My Role

Public Relations Analyst (Team Member)

Conducted crisis timeline research, analysed media coverage and public sentiment, evaluated DR’s official communication strategy, and developed recommendations to improve crisis response, stakeholder communication, and long-term brand reputation management.

Background

In September 2022, DR faced widespread criticism after reports claimed its rings cost approximately RMB 4,000 to produce but were sold for around RMB 15,000. The crisis escalated when rumours suggested purchase records could be deleted for a fee, directly challenging the brand’s core promise of lifelong commitment. The controversy generated multiple trending topics on Weibo and attracted significant public attention.

Insight

The crisis demonstrated that consumers expect transparency, accountability, and timely communication during reputation crises. When a brand’s response focuses primarily on defending its positioning rather than addressing public concerns, consumer trust can deteriorate rapidly despite strong brand awareness.

Objective

Evaluate DR’s crisis communication performance, identify weaknesses in its public relations response, and propose practical recommendations to strengthen crisis preparedness, stakeholder engagement, and brand credibility in future reputation challenges.

Strategy

Applied crisis communication and stakeholder relationship theories to examine DR’s response across different stages of the crisis. The analysis focused on response speed, message transparency, public sentiment, and relationship management to identify opportunities for more effective crisis communication and reputation recovery.

Tactics

1.Conducted a comprehensive analysis of the crisis timeline by tracking key media reports, official statements, and social media discussions to understand how public opinion evolved.
2.Evaluated DR’s official responses, identifying issues such as delayed communication, defensive messaging, and insufficient stakeholder engagement that weakened public trust.
3.Developed strategic recommendations, including establishing a dedicated crisis management team, strengthening issue monitoring, improving transparency, and shifting brand communication towards long-term trust building and responsible stakeholder engagement.
